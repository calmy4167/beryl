Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop web application direction comp, 1440x900 landscape
Primary request: Design CALMY as “编辑工作台 / Editorial Workbench”, a premium Chinese personal reflection product. This is a real operating screen, not a landing page, mood board, poster, or analytics dashboard. The user briefly enters while mentally overloaded, identifies today’s single most important real-world matter, writes one first step, records a thought, then leaves.
Input images: attached screenshots are visual craft references only. Match their excellent product proportions, refined spacing, clean light surfaces, confident color restraint, and finish. Do not copy their avatars, charts, metrics, illustrations, business content, or card arrangements.
Structure: complete 1440x900 first viewport. One single-layer pale sidebar about 184px wide with CALMY, 今天 selected, 记录, 处境, 回顾, a compact 功能 disclosure, settings at bottom. No dark icon rail and no second sidebar. Main area is an OPEN editorial canvas rather than a panel grid. A slim utility bar sits at top with date, search, connection status; no avatar.
Composition: treat today as a modern magazine front page being edited. Large heading 今天, a small sentence “抓住此刻最重要的事，然后回到现实。”. The main priority appears as the single lead headline on a wide open layout, with a concise supporting line and one solid action “写下第一步”. A thin vertical rule creates a margin for small annotations, not a right card. “快速记录” is a compact editorial note field embedded beneath the lead. “今天的记录” is a three-entry text list aligned like editorial briefs with times and tiny semantic color ticks. Four secondary tools 身体状态, 思考, 轨迹, 主动放下 appear as quiet inline index links near the page edge, not large tiles.
Visual world: optical cool white, pale blue-gray canvas, graphite type, cobalt editorial marker, one muted coral proofing mark and one amber annotation. Crisp geometric Chinese sans-serif, bold but not oversized. Use thin rules, editorial alignment, subtle surface shifts, precise 8px grid, restrained 10-14px radii only where controls require them. Almost no drop shadows. One broad open surface with strong hierarchy; no nested cards.
Signature: the current priority reads like the page’s one lead story, unmistakable at a glance. Small proofing marks and color tabs signal status with professional restraint.
Text (verbatim): “CALMY”, “今天”, “记录”, “处境”, “回顾”, “功能”, “抓住此刻最重要的事，然后回到现实。”, “此刻最重要的事情”, “写下第一步”, “快速记录”, “今天的记录”, “身体状态”, “思考”, “轨迹”, “主动放下”.
Constraints: production-ready product UI; straight-on orthographic screenshot; crisp readable Chinese; realistic interaction affordances; enough density to feel complete.
Avoid: dark navigation rail, dual sidebar, bento grid, rounded white-card pile, dashboards, charts, KPI metrics, avatars, decorative illustrations, purple gradients, glassmorphism, huge marketing hero, excessive icons, floating pill overload, watermark.
