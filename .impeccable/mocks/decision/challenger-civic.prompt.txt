Heading 今天 with “抓住此刻最重要的事，然后回到现实。” A single continuous soft ribbon of teal to lilac to violet begins as the selected indicator in the sidebar, passes lightly through the top of the canvas, and settles behind ONE large current-focus region. One saturated violet action only. The ribbon is the single memorable material; everything else is calm and almost flat. No scattered multicolor icon boxes.
Composition: asymmetrical, luxurious, practical, with one focal task and no repeated card matrix. It must read immediately as a production-ready personal work app.
